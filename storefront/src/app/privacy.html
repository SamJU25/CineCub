Privacy Policy

Welcome to StreamHeaven. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you use our website and mobile application.

Summary: We collect minimal data solely to sync your watchlist, resume playback timestamps, and manage optional accounts. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use StreamHeaven, we may store:

  • Authentication Data: When you optionally sign in (via Google or email), we store your display name, email, and encrypted authentication tokens provided securely by Firebase.
  • Watchlist & Playback Progress: Content titles you add to your watchlist, along with playback resume timestamps, so you can continue watching seamlessly across your devices.
  • Preferences: Custom theme preferences and playback settings saved locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used to:

  • Provide instant synchronization of your watchlist and watch history.
  • Enable seamless cloud resume across web, Android, and tablets.
  • Maintain and protect the security and reliability of our platform.

3. Data Retention & Control

You maintain 100% control over your data. At any time from your Profile Settings, you can:

  • Clear Watch History: Wipe all stored playback timestamps and history entries.
  • Clear Watchlist: Remove all saved bookmarked titles.
  • Delete Account: Permanently erase your account, profile, and all synced data from our servers.

4. Third-Party Integrations

We retrieve movie and TV series metadata and poster artwork using the TMDB API. Video streaming sources are provided by independent third parties.
