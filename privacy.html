Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Welcome to StreamHeaven. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website/app and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

1. Important Information and Who We Are

StreamHeaven is an independent streaming platform. We collect minimal information to provide you with the best possible service, such as maintaining your watchlist and watch history playback progress.

2. The Data We Collect About You

We may collect, use, store and transfer different kinds of personal data about you which we have grouped together as follows:

  • Identity Data: Includes username or similar identifier, and encrypted authentication details provided by third-party sign-in providers (e.g., Google) or your email if you create a local account.
  • Usage Data: Includes information about how you use our app, the titles you add to your watchlist, and your watch history with playback resume timestamps. This data is synced to the cloud to provide a seamless cross-device experience. You have full control to clear your history and watchlist anytime in your Profile Settings.

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We will only use your personal data when the law allows us to. Most commonly, we will use your personal data in the following circumstances:

  • To manage your account and authentication status.
  • To synchronize your watchlist, watch history, and playback progress across your devices.
  • To improve our app, services, and user experiences.

4. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used or accessed in an unauthorised way, altered or disclosed. We use secure cloud infrastructure (Firebase) to store your authenticated data.

5. Your Legal Rights

Under certain circumstances, you have rights under data protection laws in relation to your personal data, including the right to request access, correction, erasure, restriction, transfer, to object to processing, to portability of data and (where the lawful basis of processing is consent) to withdraw consent.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at support@streamheaven.online.
